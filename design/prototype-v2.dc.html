Every tea has a keeper.

At the Academy, the deep tea is kept by one cat and the bright tea by another, each with a single treasured object and a favourite corner of the grounds. There is a conservatory where the plants winter, a library window where recipes collect in the margins, a garden bridge for slow morning cups and a tea room where every tin is tasted before it is shelved. The words wrap around the window on purpose: the world is soft and painted, but the shop stays clear, so you always know what is in the tin, what it tastes like and when it will reach your door.
