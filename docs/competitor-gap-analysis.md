# Competitor Sitemaps and Gap Analysis: WITCHA MATCHA Commerce Website

Researched 12 October 2026 · Method: page-text extraction of each site's home page, one collection page and one product page (no screenshots). Interaction details such as hover zoom, sticky bars and animation could not be confirmed and are marked "not confirmed". Prices are AUD (Rocky's is USD). Companion to `01_Claude-Design-Prompt_Witcha-Matcha-Commerce-Site.md`.

Sites reviewed: With Miles (withmiles.com.au) · Rocky's Matcha (rockysmatcha.com) · Matcha Society (matchasociety.com.au) · Hello Matcha (hello-matcha.com.au)

## 5. Product page anatomy

| Element | With Miles (Neko 30g, $52) | Rocky's (Ceremonial 20g, $36) | Matcha Society (Ceremonial Uji 30g, $46) | Hello Matcha (Ceremonial 50g, $39.90) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gallery | 8 images | 5 images + thumbnails | 4 (tin, swatch, flavour profile, latte) | about 16 incl. infographic panels |
| Subtitle / tagline | Tasting-notes line | None | "signature ceremonial blend" | Benefit-led subtitle |
| Rating near title | Review count link | 4.9 from 455 | **Not near title** (only in reviews section) | "Rated 4.9/5" link |
| Price extras | Struck-through price equals price (bug) | "Save" with no amount | Regular = sale price; blank unit price | **Per-cup cost shown** |
| Variants | One size | 20 g / 100 g (separate page) | None visible | Retail 50 g / Bulk 1 kg |
| Quantity | Not captured | Yes | Yes | Stepper |
| Shipping info by the button | **No** (accordion later) | **No** (banner only) | **No** (sections below; conflicting times) | **Yes** (cost and days) |
| Taste visual | Text "Flavour Profile" list | None | Flavour image | None |
| Origin facts | Dedicated section + icons (notes, cultivar, harvest) | In description | Bullets (harvest, cultivar, origin) | In description |
| How to prepare | Accordion: 3 g, 40 mL, 80 °C | Not on page (Matcha Guide link) | Latte and usucha recipes, measured | Brewing guide (latte) |
| Storage | Not captured | No | Yes, specific (refrigerate, reach room temperature) | No |
| Reviews | Count + distribution + photos + Load more; no filters | AI summary, filter, 6 sorts, verified badge, helpful votes | 4.9 / 48 carousel | 4.9 / 87, AI summary block, Q&A heading |
| Related products | Not labelled | "You may also like" | Only inside out-of-stock notice | "We Think You'll Also Love…" (8) |
| Sticky add to cart | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed | Not confirmed |
| Pay-later messaging | Afterpay (lower on page) | n/a | n/a | n/a |

**Readability problems seen (avoid):** contradictory stock text (With Miles: "in stock", "only 0 left", "out of stock" on one page); sold-out notice beside an active Add to Cart (Matcha Society); conflicting dispatch times (5–10 vs 2 business days); rating separated from the title; placeholder text left on page; duplicated media; unlabelled related blocks; 30-day guarantee limited to 50 g packs but easy to miss (Hello Matcha).

**What works (borrow):** measured prep recipes (Matcha Society); delivery cost and days by the button and per-cup cost (Hello Matcha); labelled accordions and a flavour list (With Miles); review filtering, sorting and verified badges (Rocky's).

## 8. Gap analysis: where Witcha can be clearly better

| # | Gap in the market | Evidence | Witcha design response | Frames |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **No visual taste guidance.** Taste is text or a hidden filter. | With Miles filter list; Matcha Society image-only flavour profile | `TasteScale` Bright ↔ Deep on cards, PDP, home, collection header (also matches the confirmed brand line "Which Matcha Today?") | A6, C5, D1, E1 |
| 2 | **Shipping promise is hidden or inconsistent.** | Accordion-only (With Miles); 5–10 vs 2 days (Matcha Society); banner-only (Rocky's) | One `ShippingPromise` source of truth beside the CTA | E1, F1, H6 |
| 3 | **PDP data glitches** erode trust. | Struck-through equal price, blank unit price, conflicting stock text | Strict state rules for price, stock and button | E3 |
| 4 | **Prep guidance is separate or generic.** | Rocky's links to a guide; others give one recipe | `PrepMethodTabs` (whisk, frother, shaker, iced) on the PDP, with video slot | E1, H2 |
| 5 | **Cost per cup rarely shown honestly.** | Hello Matcha does it but with health claims | Per-cup line (only when serving size and price exist); no claims | E1 |
| 6 | **Cart is a pass-through.** | Meters and cross-sell appear on only two sites; gift options on none | Drawer with free-shipping meter, "Pair with", gift note, express pay | F1–F4 |
| 7 | **No sticky buy action confirmed.** | None confirmed | `StickyBuyBar` mobile and desktop | E1, E2 |
| 8 | **Trust claims do not reconcile or are fabricated-looking.** | 15,000+ customers vs 14 PDP reviews; AI-generated review summaries | Real reviews only; zero-review state designed; `SAMPLE DATA` tags in design | E1, E3 |
| 9 | **Noisy offers.** | Pop-up + footer + "up to 15%" | No pop-up; one newsletter band; no launch discounting (Brand Bible 10.6) | B4, C |
| 10 | **Heavy navigation for a small range.** | 6–7 top-level items with deep menus | Five top-level items, two levels | B1, B2 |
| 11 | **Gifting is an afterthought.** | Gift card only (With Miles) | Gifts page, gift note, delivery cut-off messaging | C8, D4, F1 |
| 12 | **Freshness and storage are rarely explained.** | Only Matcha Society | Storage block on PDP and packaging link | E1 |
| 13 | **Character-led, art-led commerce.** | With Miles / Anya already sells character blends; none builds a coherent world in the shop experience | Academy page, character panel on PDP, arch motif, empty arch placeholders until art exists | E1, H3 |

**Do not copy:** health-benefit cards (Hello Matcha) conflict with Brand Bible rules and FSANZ-style claim risk. "Trusted by 1000+ cafés", "15,000+ customers", or any award mark are not available to Witcha.
