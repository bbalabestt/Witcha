## No client wording: please approve

**Cookie banner, needs a decision.** "We use essential cookies to run the shop and keep your cart. With your OK, we also measure which pages help." · Essential only · Accept. No client file supplies this, it is legal-facing, and the site has no analytics yet.

**Plain interface labels.** These do a job rather than sell anything.
- Cart: Subtotal, Checkout, Remove, View cart, Keep shopping, Maximum (number), Your cart (number)
- Product: Size
- Search: Search, Popular searches, See all results, Results for…, Nothing for… yet, Teas
- Errors: Something went wrong, Try again, Too many requests and similar form messages
- Motion: Pause animation, Play animation
- Production controls kept from V4: View tea, Menu, Top, Back to top
- Screen readers only: Skip to content, Open menu, Close menu, Close search, Close cart, Increase or decrease quantity, Show image (number)

**Only seen once a block is switched on.** "Thank you. We'll reply by email soon." (Contact); gift and shipping lines; accessory descriptions.

**Not public.** The "Checkout preview" stand-in only appears without a connected Shopify store.
